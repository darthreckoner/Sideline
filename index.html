How it works

Pass: tap a teammate of the player with the ball. Each tap is one pass.

Move: drag any player or the ball. Drop the ball on a player to give it to them.

Player labels: positions show by default. In Move mode, hold a player for a moment to open its menu. Choose Pos and a position, or # and a number, then Apply. On a computer, right-click also works. Undo reverses a label edit.

Steal / give the ball: tap the ball, then tap any player (or an open spot).

Demonstrate: set up the play, tap Set start, drag or pass, then tap Reset. Repeat as often as you like. Undo brings the demo back.

Formation: choose a formation in Menu, or use Reset formation for a fresh board with its default position labels. GK is the goalkeeper on each team.

Draw: pick Arrow or Draw and swipe. Erase removes a line you touch.

Turn your phone sideways for a wide field. It also works upright. Open once online, then add to Home Screen for offline use. Local saves are not a guaranteed backup.
