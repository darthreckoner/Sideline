How it works

Move and pass

Choose Move, then drag a player or the ball. To pass, tap a teammate of the player with the ball. To give the ball to anyone, tap the ball and then that player. Tap an empty spot to leave it loose.

Players show their positions at first. In Move mode, hold a player, choose Pos for a position or # for a number, then tap Apply. On a computer, you can also right-click. Tap Undo if you want to change it back.

Show a play

Tap Reset to return players and the ball to their default positions. To save your own starting point, put the players where you want them and tap Set start. Move them or pass the ball, then tap Reset to go back to your saved start. Undo brings back the play you just showed.

For arrows, choose Arrow, then pick Pass for a solid arrow or Run for a dashed one. Swipe across the field to place it. Choose Draw to sketch or Erase to remove a line.

Find an idea or set up your team

In Coaching, pick an activity. Tap How to play & source for a short description and the original lesson, or Open on board to show it. On the board, tap the activity title to see its cue and source again. When you're done, tap Default field for a fresh field in the current format. Undo brings the activity back.

On Board, choose your team and tap Show lines. Defense connects players in the same position group; Offense shows triangles between teammates. Lines follow the players as you move them. Triangles show spacing, not necessarily an open pass. Turn lines off and on to make new triangles from the current positions.

Use Teaching aids in Coaching to show guides, focus on one half or hide players. Defensive position lines are for formation boards, not activity diagrams.

A T marks a target player. OFF marks the defender who has stopped in the U-10 activity.

In Team, use Quick board setup without adding players, or make a roster and choose a lineup. Check the preview, then tap Apply to board. Editing your team leaves the board as it is.

Save and use offline

Your current board is saved on this device. Tap Save to keep a named board in My playbook, or Share to send a picture. Export your board, playbook or team to keep a backup somewhere else. Team and playbook imports make copies. Opening an imported board replaces the current board, but Undo brings it back.

Turn your phone sideways for a wider field, or use it upright. For offline use, open Sideline while you're online and check the offline status in Menu. On an iPhone or iPad, use Safari's Share menu to add Sideline to your Home Screen.
